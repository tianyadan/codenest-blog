# The Three Layers of Sincerity

## Main Content

Something I thought of today. Writing it down. Maybe a motto for life.

Sincerity has three layers.

Surface --- basic manners and facts ---- be sincere with everyone. That is character.

Middle --- opinions, likes, emotions --- be sincere with the friends you choose. That is closeness.

Deep --- weaknesses, soft spots, core plans --- only with people who have been tested over time, and who have earned each other's trust.

If someone betrays you, what you should do is not "stop being sincere". Move them from the deep layer down to the surface. Then keep being sincere with the people who deserve it.

In the end, sincerity is for opening roads, not for building walls. If a wall falls, build a door again. Do not stop opening windows.
